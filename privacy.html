ORVIA WEB PRIVACY

What we collect and why.

ORVIA Web is a service of ORVIA Oversight Ltd. This notice supplements the wider ORVIA privacy information for the specific data handled through web enquiries and web-service delivery.

Controller

ORVIA Oversight Ltd is the data controller for ORVIA Web enquiries and customer-administration data. Company number 16123685. ICO registration ZC152311. Contact: web@orvia.org.uk.

Enquiries

When you submit the ORVIA Web enquiry form we collect your name, business name if supplied, email, telephone number if supplied, the service you are interested in and your message. We also record limited source/attribution information where available, such as the page used and campaign parameters.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess what you are asking for, prepare a quote or next step, and maintain an audit trail of business communications.

Customer projects

If you become a customer we may hold project briefs, contact details, domain information, content you supply, design preferences, service requirements, project correspondence, invoices, support records and access details needed to deliver the agreed work.

We aim to minimise retained credentials. Where possible, accounts remain owned by the customer and ORVIA is granted role-based access rather than taking permanent ownership of the account.

Payment data

Online card payments are processed by Stripe. ORVIA does not need to receive your full card number. Stripe processes payment information under its own terms and privacy arrangements.

Hosting and processors

ORVIA may use reputable technology providers for hosting, databases, deployment, email, forms, analytics, payment processing and customer systems. The exact providers used can differ by project. Where ORVIA acts as processor for customer-controlled data, the applicable contractual and data-processing arrangements will be agreed for the project as required.

Demonstration websites

Our demonstration sites are fictional environments built to show capability. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information into them. Demo forms and workflows may be simulated, browser-local or isolated from production systems depending on the demonstration. Each demo should explain its own behaviour.

Cookies and analytics

The main ORVIA Web site uses essential storage to remember your cookie choice. Optional analytics and advertising code is designed not to run until consent is given. At the date of this notice, optional tracking identifiers in the current site build are not active.

Lawful basis

The lawful basis depends on the activity. Business enquiries and ordinary customer administration are generally handled where necessary for steps toward a contract, performance of a contract, or ORVIA's legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and operating the service. Where consent is the appropriate basis — for example optional marketing cookies — we ask for it.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as reasonably required for the purpose, contractual record, legal obligations, security and dispute handling. Different categories may have different retention periods. We review unnecessary data and remove or anonymise it where it is no longer required.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to use of your personal data, and rights relating to portability or consent. Contact web@orvia.org.uk to exercise a relevant right.

Complaints about data use

Please raise the issue with ORVIA first so we can investigate it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office where applicable.

Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational controls appropriate to the service. We do not describe ICO registration as a cybersecurity certification, and we do not claim that any internet service can be made risk-free.
