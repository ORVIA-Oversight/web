Ownership and leaving

We recommend that the customer's domain and third-party accounts stay registered to the customer's business wherever practical. ORVIA is granted the access needed to complete the work.

For Own It projects, the agreed custom content and supplied custom code are handed over once the agreed payment obligations are complete. Third-party software, fonts, stock assets, plugins, APIs and platform components remain subject to their own licences.

Managed services include hosting, maintenance and the amendments defined in the chosen plan. After the minimum managed term, we can discuss handover or migration of a standard website where technically practical. Bespoke applications or third-party systems may require separately scoped migration work.

Support and amendments

“Support” means help with ORVIA-built functionality, agreed hosting/technical issues and the changes included in your plan. It does not mean unlimited redesign, unlimited content creation, new features, paid advertising, third-party licence fees or open-ended development.

A “small amendment” means a limited content change that does not materially alter the page structure or create new functionality — for example, changing text, replacing an image supplied by the client, or updating contact details. New sections, new pages, integrations, redesigns or functional changes are quoted separately.

Veteran Support Promise

ORVIA Oversight Ltd is veteran-owned and has committed to supporting the Armed Forces community. Our intention is practical support rather than marketing theatre.

  • Discount: 50% off ORVIA's own initial website build or setup element for an eligible UK Armed Forces veteran-owned business.
  • Eligibility: the eligible veteran must own or control the business purchasing the service. Regular and Reserve veterans are included. Serving personnel can contact us and we will confirm eligibility before payment.
  • Verification: we accept reasonable UK service evidence such as a Veteran Card, discharge/service documentation or another reliable form of service verification. We do not require you to publish service details.
  • Excluded costs: domains, third-party licences, software, advertising spend, telephony usage, premium assets and other pass-through costs are not discounted unless expressly stated.
  • Support: Own It veteran builds receive a 90-day defect-support period plus 30-, 90- and 180-day check-ins. Managed customers receive the support included in their plan.
  • Out of scope: new functionality, redesigns, new pages, new content production and third-party charges are quoted separately.

We reserve the right to protect delivery quality by limiting the number of discounted builds we accept at one time. Any capacity limit will be explained before payment.
