COMPLAINTS

If something is wrong,
tell us clearly.

A complaints process is not a substitute for fixing problems early. If something is not what you expected, contact us as soon as possible and give us a chance to put it right.

How to complain

Email web@orvia.org.uk with the subject “Complaint”, or call 0330 043 3703 and ask for the concern to be recorded as a formal complaint.

Please include the project/business name, what happened, what you expected to happen, relevant dates and what outcome you are seeking.

Who reviews it

Complaints about ORVIA Web are overseen by John McGill as founder/director of ORVIA Oversight Ltd. If the complaint concerns John directly, the company will arrange an appropriate independent or alternative review where proportionate to the issue.

Timescales

  • We aim to acknowledge a formal complaint within two working days.
  • We aim to provide a substantive response within five working days where the matter is straightforward.
  • If investigation will take longer, we will explain why and give an updated timescale.

What we will do

We will review the agreed scope, communications, relevant technical evidence and what was delivered. Where ORVIA made an error, the response may include correction, re-performance, explanation, service adjustment, refund for undelivered paid work or another proportionate remedy depending on the circumstances.

What this process does not remove

This process does not remove any contractual or statutory rights you may have. Data-protection complaints may also be raised with the ICO where applicable. Consumer customers may have additional statutory routes depending on the contract.

Learning

Material complaints are used to improve ORVIA's process, terms, scope definitions and service controls. Where appropriate we record corrective actions and verify whether they were actually completed.
